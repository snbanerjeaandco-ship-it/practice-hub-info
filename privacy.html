Privacy Policy - Practice Hub

S N Banerjea & Co, Chartered Accountants - last updated 6 October 2026

1. What Practice Hub is

Practice Hub is the internal practice-management software of S N Banerjea & Co ("the firm", "we"). It is used only by the firm's partners and staff, on the firm's own office computer, to prepare and keep the working papers of the professional services we render to our clients (for example GST reconciliations, income-tax and Form 26AS records, financial statements and audit working papers). It is not offered to the public.

2. Google account access and the information it uses

The firm connects Practice Hub to the firm's own Google account so that the reports it prepares are also kept in the firm's Google Drive, together with an encrypted backup of the software's data. For this, Practice Hub asks Google only for the permission "See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app" (drive.file). With it, Practice Hub can see and manage only the files and folders it creates itself. It cannot read any other file in the Google Drive, and it does not access Gmail, contacts, calendar or any other Google service. From Google it receives the account's e-mail address and the Drive storage figures, which it shows to the firm's administrators.

3. How the information is used

  • Only to copy the firm's own reports to its Google Drive and to store and test the firm's encrypted backups.
  • It is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, and it is not used for advertising or to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • Practice Hub's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Clients' information

The information of our clients that Practice Hub holds (such as PAN, GSTIN, returns and financial records) is processed only to render the professional services the client has engaged us for, under the confidentiality that binds us as Chartered Accountants and the law applicable in India. It is not disclosed to anyone except where the engagement or the law requires it.

5. Where it is kept and how it is protected

  • On the firm's office computer, and in the firm's own Google Drive.
  • Backups are encrypted (AES-256) before they leave the office computer; the key is kept by the firm.
  • Access is limited to the firm's partners and staff, each with their own login; signing in from outside the office needs a code from a phone app (two-step sign-in), and every sign-in is recorded.

6. Keeping and removing information

Reports stay in the firm's Google Drive for as long as the firm keeps them. Daily backups are kept for 14 days and one backup a month for 12 months; older backups are deleted. The firm can withdraw Practice Hub's access to Google Drive at any time (in Practice Hub, or under Google Account › Security › Third-party access); files already in the Drive then remain under the firm's control.

7. Contact

For any question about this policy or about your information, please contact the partners of S N Banerjea & Co, Chartered Accountants.
