Practice Hub

S N Banerjea & Co, Chartered Accountants

Practice Hub is the internal practice-management software of S N Banerjea & Co. It runs on the firm's own office computer and is used only by the firm's partners and staff to prepare and keep the working papers of the professional services the firm renders to its clients. It is not offered to the public, and there is nothing to sign up for here.

The firm connects Practice Hub to its own Google Drive, only to keep copies of the reports it prepares and encrypted backups of its data. Practice Hub can see only the files it creates itself in that Drive.
